Features

Unticked means gone from their menu and refused by their till — for everybody there, including the owner. The register, the catalogue, stock and the cashbox are the till itself and are always on.

Subscription

Changing this does not move the date they have paid to — a shop that has paid to March has paid to March, whatever it agrees to pay from now on.

Copies

Taken by the shop each day it is open. Restoring puts it back to one of these — a copy of where it is now is taken first, without asking.
